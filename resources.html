FAQ

Common questions.

Do I need any experience to join a course?

No. Most of our tracks, including Digital Literacy and Microsoft Office, assume zero prior experience. Intermediate tracks note any prerequisites on the course page.

Are the courses really free?

Our core tracks are free or low-cost, funded by donors and partners. Some specialised tracks may carry a small materials fee.

Do I have to become a mentor after I graduate?

It's not mandatory, but it's strongly encouraged, mentoring is how the network keeps growing without needing more outside funding per learner.

How do I become a mentor without taking a course first?

If you already have a skill worth teaching, get in touch through our contact page, we'll talk about pairing you with a cohort directly.

Can my organisation partner with PEERS-IN-TECH?

Yes, we work with schools, businesses, and other NGOs on funding, venues, and joint programs. Reach out via the contact page.

Still have questions?

Talk to us directly.

We're happy to walk you through which track fits, or how to get involved as a mentor or partner.
