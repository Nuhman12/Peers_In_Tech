Why choose us

A different way to learn tech.

Real skills, taught by people who have just walked the same road you are about to take.

Peer-led teaching

Every instructor learned the material themselves not long ago, so they know exactly where you might get stuck.

Free & low-cost

Our workshops are free, funded by donors and partners so cost is never the barrier to digital literacy.

Flexible scheduling

Online, evening, and weekend cohorts built to fit around work, school, and family commitments.

A network, not a certificate

You leave with mentors, peers, and a role in the next cohort, not just a piece of paper.
