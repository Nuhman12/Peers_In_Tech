People-powered, from day one.

PEERS IN TECH Foundation is a nonprofit organisation working to reduce digital illiteracy among the youth of Africa, through free workshops taught by peers, for peers.

Our story

Why we started PEERS IN TECH.

The world's adoption of technology is accelerating, but Africa is lagging behind, a hindrance to the continent's growth. As tech enthusiasts, we analysed the situation and came up with the idea of spurring the youth of Africa towards technology by organising free online workshops to teach digital skills. This inspired the name "PEERS IN TECH."

Our sole aim is to reduce the rate of digital illiteracy in Africa and beyond.

Mission & vision

Mission

To reduce the rate of digital illiteracy among the youth of Africa by teaching them the necessary digital skills.

Vision

To extend digital education to youth in every corner of the African continent by 2030, reaching out and purposefully opening our doors for individuals to embrace digital literacy.

Local first

Mentors teach in their own neighbourhoods, in the languages their community speaks best.
